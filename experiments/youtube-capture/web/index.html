Paste a YouTube URL. The spike temporarily downloads a short bounded clip (yt-dlp, local TTL cache), samples up to six frames with ffmpeg, and runs Perceptron Mk1.5. No tab share required.

How this works

  • yt-dlp pulls at most ~90s / ≤720p into experiments/youtube-capture/cache/.
  • Cache hits skip re-download for 1 hour (TTL).
  • ffmpeg samples stills; Perceptron sees those JPEGs only.
  • This is an explicit download path — not “link-only magic” and not tab capture.
